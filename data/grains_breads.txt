Roti (whole wheat flatbread): 1 medium roti (~40g) contains approximately 120 kcal, 3g protein, 18g carbohydrates, 3g fat, 2.5g fiber. Made from atta (whole wheat flour), water, and cooked on a tawa without oil. A staple in North Indian meals, typically eaten with dal or sabzi.

Paratha (layered flatbread): 1 medium paratha (~60g, made with ghee) contains approximately 260 kcal, 5g protein, 30g carbohydrates, 12g fat, 2g fiber. Stuffed parathas (aloo, paneer, gobi) add 50-100 kcal depending on filling. Higher fat than roti due to ghee/oil layering during cooking.

White rice (cooked): 1 cup (~150g) contains approximately 205 kcal, 4g protein, 45g carbohydrates, 0.5g fat, 0.5g fiber. Common base carbohydrate across most Indian regional cuisines, especially South and East India.

Brown rice (cooked): 1 cup (~150g) contains approximately 215 kcal, 5g protein, 45g carbohydrates, 1.5g fat, 3.5g fiber. Higher fiber and micronutrient content than white rice due to retained bran layer.

Idli (steamed rice-lentil cake): 1 medium idli (~40g) contains approximately 40 kcal, 1.5g protein, 8g carbohydrates, 0.2g fat, 0.4g fiber. Fermented batter of rice and urad dal (black gram). Low-fat, easily digestible, common South Indian breakfast.

Dosa (fermented crepe): 1 plain dosa (~80g) contains approximately 130 kcal, 3g protein, 20g carbohydrates, 4g fat, 1g fiber. Made from fermented rice and urad dal batter, cooked with a small amount of oil on a griddle.
