Samosa (fried pastry): 1 medium samosa (~60g) contains approximately 260 kcal, 4g protein, 30g carbohydrates, 14g fat, 2g fiber. Deep-fried, high in saturated fat due to oil absorption during frying. Common festive/tea-time snack.

Bhel puri (puffed rice snack): 1 cup (~100g) contains approximately 170 kcal, 4g protein, 30g carbohydrates, 4g fat, 3g fiber. Puffed rice mixed with vegetables, tamarind chutney, and sev; moderate calorie street food, lower fat than fried snacks.

Masala chai (spiced tea with milk and sugar): 1 cup (~150ml, standard preparation) contains approximately 90 kcal, 2g protein, 12g carbohydrates, 3g fat, 0g fiber. Calorie content driven primarily by added sugar (typically 1-2 tsp) and whole milk.

Lassi (sweetened yogurt drink): 1 glass (~250ml) contains approximately 180 kcal, 6g protein, 25g carbohydrates, 6g fat, 0g fiber. Made from dahi (yogurt), sugar, and water/milk; probiotic benefits from yogurt culture.

Buttermilk / chaas (spiced yogurt drink, unsweetened): 1 glass (~250ml) contains approximately 45 kcal, 3g protein, 5g carbohydrates, 1.5g fat, 0g fiber. Low-calorie alternative to lassi, often flavored with cumin, mint, and salt instead of sugar.
