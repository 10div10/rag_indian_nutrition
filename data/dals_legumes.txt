Dal (yellow lentil, cooked): 1 cup (~200g, moong or toor dal, lightly tempered) contains approximately 180 kcal, 12g protein, 28g carbohydrates, 3g fat, 8g fiber. Excellent plant protein source, forms the primary protein base in most Indian vegetarian diets.

Rajma (kidney bean curry): 1 cup (~200g) contains approximately 220 kcal, 10g protein, 35g carbohydrates, 5g fat, 9g fiber. High in fiber and iron; typically simmered in a tomato-onion gravy with moderate oil.

Chole (chickpea curry): 1 cup (~200g) contains approximately 260 kcal, 11g protein, 35g carbohydrates, 8g fat, 10g fiber. Higher fat content than rajma due to typical preparation with more oil and ghee.

Sprouted moong salad: 1 cup (~100g, no oil) contains approximately 105 kcal, 7g protein, 19g carbohydrates, 0.4g fat, 5g fiber. Raw or lightly steamed sprouted mung beans, common high-protein low-calorie snack.

Paneer (cottage cheese): 100g contains approximately 265 kcal, 18g protein, 3g carbohydrates, 20g fat, 0g fiber. High-quality dairy protein, common in North Indian vegetarian dishes (paneer tikka, palak paneer, mattar paneer).
